By comparing Figures \ref{fig:appendix1} with \ref{fig:appendix2}, one could notice that the dataset coming from NCEP is the most similar to Figure \ref{fig:appendix1}, followed by GSMaP. It is not mentioned where Figure \ref{fig:appendix1} data was taken from, but we hypothesized that it comes from the same source, since NCEP encompasses the stations and radar datasets. GPM-IMERG, however, fails to represent the extremes shown in both NCEP and GSMaP, and can’t capture some structures of the rain (85-81°W and 32-27°N) that NCEP and GSMaP can. For our analysis, we used NCEP data over the United States and GSMaP outside the United States, which seems to better represent the amount produced and some structures as well. One should keep in mind that the downscaling spreads the data, creating a systematic bias (panels A and C).
